TAINAN BAR HOP · GUIDE

🍸 府城微醺地圖使用說明

從選起點、找酒吧，到今晚續攤，一步一步就能開始。

01

設定出發地點

預設從「好的我願意台南民宿」出發。按「更換起點」即可選民宿、手機目前位置、地圖點選，或輸入完整地址／經緯度。GPS 需要瀏覽器定位權限。

02

探索和搜尋酒吧

「探索」中可輸入店名、街道或酒種類型，並篩選調酒、啤酒、餐酒館和收藏。按「附近開喝」只會篩選已有座標的店家；公里數為直線距離，不是步行距離。

03

看圖釘和店家資料

地圖上的綠色圖釘代表酒吧，房屋符號是起點；加入行程的酒吧會顯示粉橘色數字（拜訪順序）。點店家可看地址、特色及資料品質。原始店名與地址保持原文，方便在導航服務搜尋。

04

隨機挑選與收藏

按「幫我選一間」讓網站依範圍、酒種、是否已去過或已在續攤清單等條件抽選。按愛心可收藏，收藏及續攤資料保存在目前瀏覽器。

05

安排續攤與導航

按「＋ 續攤」把酒吧加入路線；「我的續攤」可移動順序、依直線距離自動排序，並開啟 Google Maps 步行導航。「導航下一站」以目前位置出發；實際路線以 Google Maps 為準。

06

今晚開喝與分享

使用「今晚」模式逐站查看目的地，標記完成或跳過；可隨時退出。按「分享可還原網址」把行程發給朋友；分享網址不包含你當時的 GPS 定位，但可能含所選酒吧與行程順序。

常見問題

為什麼有些店沒有距離或圖釘？

店家沒有可靠座標時，不會偽造位置或距離。精選店家的政府門牌座標是參考點，未必是店門口。你可開店家資訊、Google Maps 或用地圖手動確認。

酒吧清單是臺南所有營業酒吧嗎？

不是。網站整合精選清單、OpenStreetMap 與公開點位，可能有漏標、停業或搬遷。出發前請核對店家最新營業資訊和是否接受訂位。

「座標品質自動比較」代表什麼？

網站比較 OSM 原始座標與臺南市政府門牌參考座標，顯示偏差與資料是否一致；它不能保證實際入口的正確位置，也不能保證店家仍營業。

我的資料會同步到別的手機嗎？

不會。收藏、路線和已去過的資料預設保存在這台裝置的瀏覽器，清除網站資料可能讓它們消失。分享行程則會產生可還原的網址。

資料來源與安全提醒

底圖與酒吧公開點位：© OpenStreetMap contributors；部分點位經 ArcGIS／Overpass 取得。門牌參考位置：臺南市政府門牌座標開放資料。資料不保證完整、即時或是精確店門口。

請遵守臺灣法定飲酒年齡（18 歲以上），飲酒勿開車或騎車；可步行、搭計程車或使用大眾運輸。需要回程時直接開啟導航。

🍸 How to use TainanBarHop

Choose your starting point, find a bar, build a route, and enjoy your night out.

01

Choose a starting point

The default is 好的我願意台南民宿 guesthouse. Choose a different start using your GPS, a map tap, a street address, or latitude/longitude. GPS requires browser location permission.

02

Search and explore

Search by business name, street, or bar type. Filter for cocktails, beer, bistros, and favorites. “Bars nearby” only includes venues with valid coordinates. Distances are straight-line estimates, not walking distances.

03

Understand map markers

Green dots are bars and the house icon marks your starting point. Numbered peach markers show the planned stop order. Tap a bar for its address, description and coordinate quality. Business names and addresses stay in their original language so you can find them in navigation apps.

04

Lucky pick and favorites

Use “Pick for me” to choose a random bar by radius or city-wide list, bar category, and exclusions for visited or already-planned stops. The heart button saves a favorite locally.

05

Plan a bar-hop route

Choose “Add stop” to plan your night. In “My route,” reorder your stops or auto-sort by straight-line distance. Open Google Maps for walking directions. “Navigate next stop” uses your current position; Google Maps determines the actual route.

06

Tonight mode and sharing

In Tonight mode, move through each stop, mark it done or skip it. You can exit at any time. Share a route link so friends can restore the same itinerary. The link does not include your live GPS location, but it can include your chosen bars and their order.

Frequently asked questions

Why do some venues have no distance or map pin?

Unverified locations are not given fabricated coordinates or distances. Government doorplate coordinates are reference points and may not match the entrance. Check the venue in Google Maps or manually verify its pin.

Does this list include every operating bar in Tainan?

No. It combines curated picks, OpenStreetMap, and other public points. Bars may be missing, moved, or closed. Confirm hours, reservations and current business status before heading out.

What does automatic coordinate comparison tell me?

It compares original OSM points against Tainan municipal doorplate reference points. Agreement or disagreement does not prove the true entrance location or current operating status.

Are my favorites and routes synced across devices?

No. Favorites, routes, and visited status are stored in your current browser. Clearing site data may remove them. Shared itinerary links can restore a route on another device.

Data and safety

Map and public venue data: © OpenStreetMap contributors, with some points retrieved through ArcGIS / Overpass. Doorplate locations: Tainan City Government open data. Listings are not guaranteed to be exhaustive, current, or entrance-accurate.

Alcohol is for adults 18+ in Taiwan. Never drive or ride after drinking. Walk, take public transportation, or use a taxi. Use navigation for your return trip.
